Eligibility

Who can join

  • At least 21 years of age on the date of joining.
  • Committed to contributing toward the Foundation's aims and objectives.
  • Not undischarged insolvent or of unsound mind.
  • Not convicted of a morally turpitudinous offence carrying a sentence of one year or more.
  • Willing to pay the applicable entry fee and stay current on annual subscription.

How Applications Work

New members submit a membership application with ID proof to the Foundation's main office. The Secretary reviews the application and places it before the governing body, which may accept or decline it — a decision that is final and need not be explained. Approved members are recorded in the statutory register of members and issued an identity card.
