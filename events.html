Foundation News

11 June 2026

Sanatan Seva Foundation Officially Registered

The Foundation received its Certificate of Registration (No. HR-009-2026-00718) from the Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana, formally establishing it as a society under the Haryana Registration & Regulation of Societies Act, 2012. Our founding team is now building out gaushala infrastructure, finalising our donation channels, and preparing our first community programmes.

This page will be updated regularly with reports from our events, plantation drives, and gau seva milestones. Check back soon, or follow up directly with our office for the latest activity.
