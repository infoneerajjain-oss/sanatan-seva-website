Volunteer With Us

No pay, no politics — just seva. Our entire governing body serves without pay, and we welcome hands that want to do the same.

Ways to Help

Where volunteers make the biggest difference

Gaushala Support

Help with feeding, cleaning and basic care at gau seva sites, or assist coordinating rescue and ambulance response.

Events & Bhandara

Support the organisation of havan, bhandara, festival programmes, and 26 January / 15 August events.

Education & Outreach

Assist with primary education support, skill-development sessions, and public awareness campaigns on gau seva and social issues.

Environment Drives

Join tree plantation days and cleanliness drives around gaushala premises and village water bodies.

Community Welfare

Support outreach to women, elders and the unemployed within our service area.

Admin & Records

Help with documentation, accounts support, and preparing the annual returns our bye-laws require.
