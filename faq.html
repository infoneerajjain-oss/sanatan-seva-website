Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for donors, volunteers, members and partner organisations.

About the Foundation

Is Sanatan Seva Foundation a legally registered organisation?

Yes. We are registered as a society under the Haryana Registration & Regulation of Societies Act, 2012 (Registration No. HR-009-2026-00718, dated 11 June 2026). You can verify this on the official Haryana Government verification portal, or view our documents on the Transparency page.

Where is the Foundation based, and where does it work?

Our registered office is in Safidon, Jind, Haryana. Under our Memorandum of Association, our jurisdiction covers all districts of Haryana.

Does the Foundation support any political party?

No. Objective 19 of our Memorandum states explicitly that the society will stay away from politics in all its work.

Donations

How is my donation used?

Donations fund our gau seva, temple, education and community welfare programmes as described in our charter. No governing-body member, office-bearer or volunteer is paid any salary, bonus or dividend, as set out in our bye-laws.

Can I get a tax deduction for my donation?

We are in the process of applying for 12A and 80G registration with the Income Tax Department. Once granted, donation receipts will note the applicable certificate numbers. See our Donate page for current status.

Can I donate land or cattle instead of money?

Yes — gau daan (cattle donation) and bhoomi daan (land donation) are explicitly recognised under Objective 13 of our Memorandum. Contact us to arrange this.

Do you accept corporate CSR or government grants?

Yes. We welcome CSR and government partnerships, particularly for gaushala infrastructure and the mobile ambulance project. Reach out via our Contact page and we'll share our documentation for review.

Membership & Volunteering

How do I become a member?

Membership is open to individuals aged 21 and above who meet the eligibility criteria in our bye-laws. See the Membership page for categories, fees and how to apply.

Do I need to be a member to volunteer?

No. Volunteering is open to anyone who wants to contribute time or skills; formal membership is a separate, optional step for those who want a say in the Foundation's governance.

Are office-bearers and volunteers paid?

No. Our bye-laws require that all governing-body members serve without pay, and nobody may spend on the society's behalf out of pocket — expenses are reimbursed only from the society's own funds.
