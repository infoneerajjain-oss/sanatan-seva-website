In line with our bye-laws (Rule 8.2(f)), no office-bearer or executive member draws any salary or honorarium for their role. The governing body's current term runs for three years from the date of election approval by the District Registrar.

How Leadership Is Chosen

Elected, not appointed

Under our bye-laws, the governing body is elected by the general body of members, with elections conducted by a Returning Officer appointed for the purpose. The District Registrar may appoint an election observer on request. Proxy voting is not permitted — every member casts their own vote.
