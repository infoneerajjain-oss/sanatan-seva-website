Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Last updated: August 2026

Privacy Policy

Sanatan Seva Foundation ("the Foundation", "we", "us") respects the privacy of everyone who visits this website or contacts us.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to give us — for example, your name, phone number, email address and message content when you use our contact, volunteer or membership forms. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising scripts on this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to donation, volunteering, membership or partnership enquiries.
  • To maintain our statutory register of members, where applicable, as required under the Haryana Registration & Regulation of Societies Act, 2012.
  • To issue donation acknowledgements and, once available, tax-exemption receipts.

How We Protect Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. Information shared with us is accessible only to our office bearers and executive committee for the purposes above.

Your Choices

You may ask us at any time to correct or delete personal information you have shared with us, by writing to admin.sanatansevafoundation@gmail.com, except where we are legally required to retain records (such as the statutory member register).
