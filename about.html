About Sanatan Seva Foundation

A society born in Safidon, Jind — built to serve cows, temples and communities across Haryana.

Our Story

From a local gaushala concern to a state-wide charter

Sanatan Seva Foundation began with a simple, deeply felt concern: too many cows in and around Safidon and Jind were going without food, shelter or medical care. A small group of local social workers — led by our Pradhan, Shri Pardeep Kumar — came together to formalise that concern into an organisation.

On 11 June 2026, the Foundation was formally registered with the Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana under the Haryana Registration & Regulation of Societies Act, 2012 (Registration No. HR-009-2026-00718), with its registered office at S.D. No. J21, Ward No. 8, Near Power House, Safidon, Jind, Haryana – 126112.

What started as gau seva has, in our Memorandum of Association, grown into a 26-point charter spanning temple construction, education, skill development, disaster response for animals, environmental work, and support for women, the elderly and the unemployed — while remaining, by our own rule, entirely apolitical.

Vision & Values

What guides our work

Vision

A Haryana where no cow goes hungry, unsheltered or untreated, and where dharmik and social service reach every village, not just the well-connected ones.

Mission

To operate gaushalas, support dharmik infrastructure, and deliver education, health and livelihood support — funded transparently and run without personal profit to anyone involved.

Values

Seva before self, transparency in accounts, respect regardless of caste or creed (Rule ‘घ’), and staying out of party politics (Objective 19).

Legal Standing

Constituted, not improvised

Every part of how we operate — membership, elections, meetings, fund management and dissolution — is set out in our Rules & Regulations (bye-laws), approved by all founding members on 10 June 2025 and filed with the District Registrar of Societies, Jind.

Our governing body's term is capped at three years, elections are conducted by a Returning Officer, and our accounts must be audited annually — the same accountability structure any government department or corporate CSR desk would expect before funding a partner.
