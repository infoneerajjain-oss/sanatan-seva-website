Why We Exist

Rooted in gau seva, growing into full community service

Sanatan Seva Foundation was registered on 11 June 2026 under the Haryana Registration & Regulation of Societies Act, 2012, with its founding purpose in gau raksha — sheltering, feeding and treating cows abandoned or injured across Haryana. From that foundation, our charter extends into temple-building, education, skill development, disaster relief for animals, and support for women, elders and the unemployed.

Gau Seva & Rescue

Shelter, fodder and medical care for stray, injured and aged cows, with a planned mobile ambulance for accident and road-injury cases.

Temples & Dharmik Karya

Construction and upkeep of temples and community halls; havan, puja, bhandara and satsang that keep tradition alive.

Community Welfare

Support and livelihood assistance for women, girls, the elderly and the unemployed, alongside education and skill-building.

Our Approach

Every rupee works toward the cause — never toward profit

Our bye-laws are explicit: the Foundation's property and income exist solely for the good of society. No member, office-bearer or volunteer draws a salary, bonus or dividend of any kind. Anyone who spends on the society's work is reimbursed only from the society's own income, and any surplus is ploughed straight back into our programmes.

  • All governing-body positions are unpaid (Rule 8.2(f) of our bye-laws).
  • Accounts are audited annually by a Chartered Accountant.
  • All receipts and payments move through bank instruments — DD, cheque, RTGS or UPI.
  • The Foundation stays entirely out of politics (Objective 19 of our Memorandum).

A Registered, Rule-Bound Society

Sanatan Seva Foundation is registered with the Department of Industries & Commerce, Haryana under Registration No. HR-009-2026-00718 (Unique ID 2000168398), dated 11 June 2026, at Jind.

Governed by a Memorandum of Association, Bye-laws, and a three-member elected office — Pradhan, Sachiv and Koshadhyaksh — supported by a seven-member executive committee.
