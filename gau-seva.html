Gau Seva Programs

Our founding cause, and still the heart of everything we do.

The Need

Why cow welfare, and why now

Across Safidon, Jind and neighbouring districts, ageing and abandoned cows are commonly left to fend for themselves — scavenging near roads, going without treatment when injured, and facing an undignified end. Our Memorandum of Association names gau seva as the Foundation's core purpose: to provide shelter, food, medical care and a dignified final resting process for cows in need, and to raise public awareness of why this matters.

We build this work on land access, community donations, and volunteer effort — and we are actively seeking government support, CSR partnerships and individual donors to scale it further.

What “Gau Seva” Covers, By Charter

  • Open land and shelter for a natural, protected life
  • Food and relief support wherever needed
  • Medical treatment and a final shelter in old age
  • Respectful, dignified handling after death
  • Public awareness and motivation for gau seva
  • Research into nutrition and cattle health
Our Programs

Four pillars of gau seva

Shelter & Feeding

Securing open land and building basic shelter infrastructure so cows have relief from heat, cold and hunger, with fodder support sustained through donations.

Medical Treatment

Arranging veterinary care for sick, injured and elderly cows, and providing a final, cared-for shelter in old age.

Rescue & Ambulance

Responding to cows injured in road accidents or traffic incidents, with a mobile ambulance service planned as our next major infrastructure project.

Land, Water & Green Cover

Tree plantation, grazing-land conservation and cleanliness drives on gaushala premises to keep the environment healthy for the herd.
